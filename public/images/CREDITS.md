## Notes

- The Naujan Lake photographs are general views of the lake and park; they are
  not tied to a specific tour package or zone.
- `ducklings.jpg` shows the Philippine Duck (*Anas luzonica*), a species
  recorded at Naujan Lake. The photograph itself was taken elsewhere in the
  Philippines.
- **Species photographs are illustrative, not field records.** Most were taken
  outside Naujan Lake, sometimes of the same species in another part of its
  range. They identify the organism; they do not document a sighting at the
  park. Replace them with park-owned photographs as the park office supplies
  them — the caption on each detail page says so explicitly.
- Where a licence requires share-alike, re-publishing these files as resized
  WebP keeps the same licence. Attribution (author, licence, source link) is
  rendered on the catalogue cards and species detail pages.
